STEP BY STEP

Organize files in five simple steps

01
📁
STEP 01

Select your files

Open the DropSort organizer and select one or multiple files from your device.

💡 You can select multiple files at once.
02
➕
STEP 02

Add more files

Need to include more files? Use + Add More Files without losing your existing selection.

💡 You can build your complete file set before starting the process.
03
🔍
STEP 03

Review your selection

Review the selected files before processing. You can remove individual files or clear the entire selection.

💡 Check the file count before clicking Organize Files.
04
♻️
STEP 04

Choose duplicate handling

Decide what DropSort should do when identical files are detected.

📦 Move
🗑️ Skip
📁 Keep
🔄 Rename
05
📦
STEP 05

Organize and download

Click Organize Files. DropSort uploads the files, detects duplicates, classifies them, organizes them, and generates the final ZIP archive.

✓ Your organized ZIP is ready to download when processing finishes.
